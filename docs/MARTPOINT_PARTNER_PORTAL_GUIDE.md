# MartPoint Partner Portal Guide

## Login

- Partners log in at `/partner/login` with email and password.
- Forgotten passwords can be reset from the login page.
- Inactive, suspended or disabled accounts are denied.

## Profile

- View and update the partner organisation profile.
- Keep contact details and location current.

## Team

- Invite and manage partner users.
- Only active users can access the portal.
- User roles control permissions.

## Compliance

- Upload required compliance documents.
- View status of submitted documents.
- Partners cannot verify their own compliance.
- Internal notes are not visible.

## Resources

- Access shared resources provided by MartPoint.

## Lead Registration

- Register new leads from **Leads**.
- Leads are protected by MartPoint before being worked.
- Do not register leads for businesses already assigned to another partner.

## Lead Protection

- MartPoint reviews leads and marks them as protected, duplicate, or matched.
- Only protected leads can be progressed.

## Assigned Customers

- View assigned businesses from **Customers**.
- Access depends on the partner assignment and access level.
- Revoked or expired assignments remove access immediately.

## Onboarding

- For partners with onboarding capability, complete onboarding steps.
- Training records can be added.
- MartPoint reviews and approves onboarding before go-live.

## Support

- Handle assigned first-line support tickets if the organisation has `FIRST_LINE_SUPPORT`.
- Cannot view or manage sensitive categories (Billing, Licensing, Security, Privacy, Partner Complaint).
- Cannot see internal notes.
- Escalate to MartPoint when needed.

## Commissions

- View commission statements and payout status.
- Commissions are calculated and approved by MartPoint.

## Important Rules

- MartPoint controls deployment, licensing, payment confirmation, and pricing.
- Partner access is permission-based and can be revoked.
- Do not attempt to access records outside your assignment.

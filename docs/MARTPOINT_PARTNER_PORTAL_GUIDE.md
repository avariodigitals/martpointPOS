# MartPoint Partner Portal Guide

## Login

- Partners log in at `/partner/login` with email and password.
- Forgotten passwords can be reset from the login page.
- Inactive, suspended or disabled accounts are denied.

## Profile

- View and update the partner organisation profile.
- Keep contact details and location current.

## Portal navigation and module guide

After sign-in, use the left sidebar on desktop. On a phone, use the bottom quick menu and open **More** for the full menu. Navigation is filtered by the partner user role and the capabilities enabled for the organisation, so not every user will see every module.

- **Dashboard:** partner status, capability summary, compliance actions, key counts, and recent activity.
- **Leads:** register customer opportunities and follow lead status, protection, and matching decisions.
- **Referrals:** review accepted referral opportunities and decision history.
- **Quotes:** submit a quote request for a protected lead, then review, download, and accept or decline the linked Finance quote when Sales issues it.
- **Customers:** view assigned customer records and permitted customer information. Open Finance after an invoice is issued to submit payment proof for Finance verification.
- **Work Orders:** track approved implementation work, milestones, due dates, and evidence.
- **Earnings:** view commissions, statements, payout details, and payout requests.
- **Profile:** maintain organisation details, contacts, and approved logo.
- **Team:** invite and manage partner users when the current role has permission.
- **Compliance:** submit requested compliance information and track review status; MartPoint performs verification.
- **Documents:** preview or download agreements, schedules, activation records, assignments, statements, and notices issued to the partner.
- **Training:** open training materials, demonstrations, videos, and downloadable guides.
- **Certification:** view certification requirements and certificates issued or uploaded by MartPoint.
- **Assessments:** open assigned assessments, complete papers, and review assessment status.
- **Branded Materials:** download approved co-branded materials and submit branding requests where available.
- **Badge Kit:** download partner badge assets issued for the organisation.
- **Resources:** access shared MartPoint guides and documents.
- **Support Tickets:** raise and track written support requests and reply in the ticket thread.

If a module is not visible, ask your organisation administrator or MartPoint to confirm the required permission or capability. Do not use another person's account.

## Team

- Invite and manage partner users.
- Only active users can access the portal.
- User roles control permissions.

## Compliance

- Upload required compliance documents.
- View status of submitted documents.
- Partners cannot verify their own compliance.
- Internal notes are not visible.

## Resources

- Access shared resources provided by MartPoint.
- For a protected lead, open **Quotes** and select **Request quote**. Wait for Sales to issue the linked Finance quote, then open it to download and record acceptance or decline.
- After an accepted quote is converted to an issued invoice, open the assigned customer **Finance** screen to submit the payment amount, bank reference, and proof document. The submission remains pending until MartPoint Finance confirms it.

## Lead Registration

- Register new leads from **Leads**.
- Leads are protected by MartPoint before being worked.
- Do not register leads for businesses already assigned to another partner.

## Lead Protection

- MartPoint reviews leads and marks them as protected, duplicate, or matched.
- Only protected leads can be progressed.

## Assigned Customers

- View assigned businesses from **Customers**.
- Access depends on the partner assignment and access level.
- Revoked or expired assignments remove access immediately.

## Onboarding

- For partners with onboarding capability, complete onboarding steps.
- Training records can be added.
- MartPoint reviews and approves onboarding before go-live.

## Support

- Handle assigned first-line support tickets if the organisation has `FIRST_LINE_SUPPORT`.
- Cannot view or manage sensitive categories (Billing, Licensing, Security, Privacy, Partner Complaint).
- Cannot see internal notes.
- Escalate to MartPoint when needed.

## Commissions

- View commission statements and payout status.
- Commissions are calculated and approved by MartPoint.

## Important Rules

- MartPoint controls deployment, licensing, payment confirmation, and pricing.
- Partner access is permission-based and can be revoked.
- Do not attempt to access records outside your assignment.
